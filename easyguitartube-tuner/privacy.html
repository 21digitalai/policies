Privacy Policy

EasyGuitarTube - Guitar Tuner

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Overview

EasyGuitarTube - Guitar Tuner (the “App”) is a guitar tuning app. The App processes the microphone audio needed for tuning locally on your device. We do not operate accounts, analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking in the App.

Microphone and audio

When you use a tuner screen, the App listens to your instrument in real time to estimate pitch. Audio is processed on your device and discarded immediately. It is not recorded, saved, or sent to 21Digital or another party.

Data stored on your device

The App stores your selected and custom tunings, instrument setups, tuning preferences, theme and gauge settings, and similar app preferences in local app storage. This information is not transmitted to us.

QR import and optional diagnostics

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a tuning-import QR code. Images are not saved or shared. If you explicitly create a diagnostic export, you choose where to save or share that file; EasyGuitarTube does not upload it to us.

One-time purchase

EasyGuitarTube Pro is an optional one-time purchase processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. The App receives an entitlement result so it can unlock Pro features. We do not receive or store your payment-card information or store-account credentials.

Information we do not collect

  • Microphone audio, camera images, contacts, location, or account-profile information.
  • Advertising identifiers, behavioral analytics, or third-party tracking data.
  • Payment-card information.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will revise the date above. For privacy questions, contact 21digitalai@gmail.com.
