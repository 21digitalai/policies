StringLab Support

StringLab is a chromatic and instrument tuner for guitar and bass.

Getting started

  • Open the app and allow microphone access when prompted — tuning needs to hear your instrument.
  • Play a string; StringLab detects the note and shows how far you are from in tune and which way to adjust.
  • Pick your instrument and tuning from the Tunings tab, or save your own custom tunings and rigs.

Common questions

  • The tuner isn't responding. Make sure microphone access is enabled in your device Settings, and that nothing else is using the mic.
  • What does StringLab Pro include? A one-time purchase unlocks the full tuning library, premium gauge styles, advanced tools, unlimited custom tunings and rigs, and removes ads — no subscription.
  • I bought Pro on another device. Use "Restore Purchases" on the Pro screen to re-activate it.
