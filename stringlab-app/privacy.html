Privacy Policy

StringLab

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Overview

StringLab ("the App") is a chromatic and instrument tuner for guitar and bass. We are committed to your privacy. This policy explains what data the App handles and where it goes. The short version: ordinary tuning processes microphone audio on your device to detect pitch and does not record, store, or transmit that audio. The App has no user accounts, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Microphone & Audio

Tuning requires access to your microphone. When you are tuning, the App listens to your instrument and analyzes the sound in real time, on your device, to estimate the pitch. In ordinary use, the audio is processed locally and discarded immediately — it is not recorded, not saved, and not sent to us or anyone else.

The App also includes optional developer diagnostic tools. If you enable diagnostic audio and start a diagnostic capture, the App can save a local WAV recording and a local diagnostic log. This setting is off by default. Raw diagnostic audio can contain private room sound. The App does not automatically transmit these files; if you choose the share action, your device's sharing interface lets you select where to send the file.

Data Stored Only on Your Device

The App stores the following locally on your device (in standard app storage). It is never transmitted to us:

  • Tunings and custom tunings you create or select.
  • Instrument rigs and setups you save.
  • App settings and preferences — reference pitch (A4), gauge style, theme, sensitivity, and similar options.
  • Optional diagnostic files — local diagnostic logs and, only when you opt in, local WAV recordings created by the developer diagnostic tools.

Camera & Tuning Transfer

The App requests camera access only when you choose to scan a tuning-import QR code. Camera images are not saved or shared by the App. You can also import or export custom tuning data using a QR code or a file; exported files contain the tunings you choose to export and are shared only when you use your device's sharing interface.

In-App Purchases

StringLab Pro is an optional one-time purchase. Purchases are processed by the applicable app store — Apple's App Store on iOS or Google Play on Android. The App receives purchase and entitlement information needed to unlock Pro features. We do not collect, see, or store your payment details, card information, Apple ID, or Google account credentials. Store handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy or Google's Privacy Policy, as applicable.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not automatically record, store, or transmit microphone audio. Optional diagnostic audio is saved locally only after you enable it and start a diagnostic capture.
  • We do not run analytics or behavioral tracking. There are no third-party analytics SDKs in the App.
  • We do not show third-party ads, use the advertising identifier (IDFA), or share data with advertisers.
  • We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, or location.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be communicated within the App or on this page.
